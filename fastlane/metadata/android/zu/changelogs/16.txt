- Uhlelo lokusebenza manje seluthola amafoni engeziwe ngokuzenzakalela, okuhlanganisa amamodeli esifunda namadivayisi abelana ngesakhiwo somndeni esisodwa.
- Ngemuva kokuthi uhlelo lokusebenza lukusize ukuthi ulande, manje usungakwazi ukulinganisa uhlelo lokusebenza ngokushesha, futhi kusukela kumenyu.
